片語與搭配詞

每個字都認得，組在一起卻看不懂；中文想得很清楚，寫出來卻是中式英文。這兩個問題的答案都是「搭配」。這一節整理學測最常用的動詞片語、介系詞搭配和動名搭配，最後修正中譯英常見的中式說法。

這一節回答同一個問題的四個面向：「哪些字一定要一起出現？」

PART 1 ・ 動詞片語

put + off 不是「放」也不是「關」

情境

Because of the typhoon, the school put off the sports day. 把 put（放）和 off（離開）的意思加起來，猜不到「延期」。

觀察

動詞片語的意思常常不是兩個字相加，必須整組記。好消息是學測反覆出現的片語數量有限，而且綜合測驗特別愛考。

規律 → 高頻動詞片語
片語例句中文
put offThe school put off the sports day.延期
carry outThe scientists carried out an experiment.執行
come up withShe came up with a great idea.想出
run out ofWe ran out of milk.用完
look intoThe police are looking into the case.調查
give upDon't give up your dream.放棄
figure outI can't figure out this question.弄懂、想出
turn downHe turned down the job offer.拒絕
take afterShe takes after her mother.長得像、個性像（長輩）
get along withI get along well with my classmates.與…相處
look up toMany kids look up to athletes.尊敬、景仰
make up forHe worked hard to make up for lost time.彌補
注意 to 的身分。look forward to、be used to、when it comes to 裡的 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing：I look forward to seeing you.
PART 2 ・ 介系詞搭配

為什麼是 depend on，不是 depend in

情境

介系詞沒有規則可以推，中文也幫不上忙：「對…負責」是 responsible for，「對…感興趣」是 interested in，「對…很驕傲」是 proud of。

規律 → 高頻搭配
搭配例句
depend on / rely onPlants depend on sunlight.
consist ofThe team consists of five members.
result in（導致）／ result from（起因於）Smoking can result in disease. / The fire resulted from a short circuit.
be responsible forWho is responsible for the mess?
be aware ofAre you aware of the risks?
be famous for / be known forTaiwan is famous for its night markets.
be proud ofMy parents are proud of me.
focus on / concentrate onFocus on the main idea first.
deal withHow do you deal with stress?
apologize to + 人 + for + 事I apologized to her for being late.
延伸

result in 和 result from 方向相反，是綜合測驗的經典陷阱。記法：in 往後走到結果，from 往回找到原因。

PART 3 ・ 動詞＋名詞

make 和 do，take 和 have

情境

中文都說「做」：做決定、做功課、做好事。英文卻分成 make a decision、do homework、do a good deed。

觀察

大致來說，make 偏向「製造出原本沒有的東西」（決定、錯誤、噪音、進步），do 偏向「執行一件工作或活動」（功課、家事、運動）。但例外很多，最可靠的方法還是整組記。

規律 → 常用動名搭配
動詞搭配的名詞
makea decision, a mistake, progress, an effort, a difference, friends, money, noise
dohomework, housework, exercise, one's best, harm, a favor, research
takea break, a photo, a test / an exam, part in, action, medicine, notes, responsibility
payattention (to), a visit (to), the bill
keep / breaka promise, a secret, a record
raisemoney, awareness, a question, children
catcha cold, a bus / a train, someone's attention
PART 4 ・ 中式英文修正

中譯英最常見的十個中式說法

情境

中譯英是「按語意單位」給分，每個錯誤扣 0.5 分。一個中式搭配就可能讓整個詞組失分，而且這類錯誤自己最難發現。

規律 → 改掉直翻的習慣
中文中式寫法正確寫法
開燈／關燈open / close the lightturn on / turn off the light
吃藥eat medicinetake medicine
看書see a bookread a book
學習知識learn knowledgegain / acquire knowledge
參加考試join an examtake an exam
參加活動join in a clubjoin a club / take part in an event
打電話給他call to himcall him
討論這件事discuss about itdiscuss it / talk about it
他很高（身高）His height is very tall.He is very tall.
交通很擁擠The traffic is very crowded.The traffic is heavy. / The road is crowded.
檢查方法。寫完中譯英，把每個動詞和它後面的名詞挑出來問：「英文真的這樣搭嗎？」不確定時，換一個更基本、確定正確的說法。
練習

動手試試看

Q1選出最適合的片語：

We have ______ paper. Can you buy some on your way home?

  1. run out of
  2. put off
  3. looked into
  4. given up
看解答
A。紙「用完了」才需要買。have run out of 是現在完成式。
Q2填入 in 或 from：

The accident resulted ___ careless driving. Careless driving can result ___ serious injuries.

看解答
from／in。事故「起因於」不小心駕駛；不小心駕駛「導致」重傷。
Q3填入 make、do 或 take 的正確形式：

I ______ a lot of mistakes, but I ______ my best and ______ a short break every hour.

看解答
made／did／took（或現在式 make／do／take）。make mistakes、do one's best、take a break。
Q4改正這句：

I am looking forward to meet you next week.

看解答
I am looking forward to meeting you next week. 這裡的 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。
Q5中譯英：我們應該關掉不用的電燈來節省能源。
看解答

We should turn off the lights that are not in use to save energy.

「關燈」用 turn off，不用 close；「不用的」可寫成 that are not in use 或 unused。

本節重點
  • 動詞片語整組記，意思常不是兩個字相加。
  • look forward to、be used to 的 to 是介系詞，接 V-ing。
  • result in 指向結果，result from 指向原因。
  • 中譯英寫完，逐一檢查「動詞＋名詞」是不是英文真正的搭配。

例句皆為自編教學用。
