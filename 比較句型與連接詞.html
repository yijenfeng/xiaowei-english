比較句型與連接詞

比較句是中譯英的常客，「越來越…」「越…就越…」幾乎每年都有機會出現。連接詞則決定句子之間的關係，也牽動主詞動詞一致。這一節把兩者整理在一起。

這一節回答同一個問題的四個面向：「怎麼用英文比較兩樣東西，又怎麼把兩個想法正確地連起來？」

PART 1 ・ 原級與倍數

一樣、不如、幾倍

情境

「新體育館是舊的三倍大。」「這次考試沒有上次那麼難。」這兩句都用原級比較來寫。

規律 → 原級比較
意思句型例句
一樣…as + adj/adv + asKen runs as fast as his brother.
不如…not as / not so + adj + asThis test was not as difficult as the last one.
…的幾倍倍數 + as + adj + asThe new gym is three times as large as the old one.
…的幾倍（另一寫法）倍數 + 比較級 + thanThe new gym is three times larger than the old one.
兩倍用 twice。My room is twice as big as yours. 一半用 half：half as expensive as。
PART 2 ・ 比較級與最高級

比較的對象要一致

情境

「台北的人口比台南多。」寫成 The population of Taipei is larger than Tainan. 意思變成「台北的人口比台南（這座城市）大」。

觀察

比較的是「人口」和「人口」，不是「人口」和「城市」。後面要用 that 代替前面的名詞：The population of Taipei is larger than that of Tainan.（複數名詞用 those。）

規律 → 比較級與最高級的重點
重點例句
修飾比較級用 much / far / even / a lot，不用 veryThis phone is much cheaper than that one.
比較對象一致，用 that / those 代替The prices here are higher than those in Tainan.
最高級前加 the，範圍用 in（地方、團體）或 of（群體成員）She is the tallest girl in our class. / the best of all
不規則變化good / well → better → best；bad → worse → worst；many / much → more → most；little → less → least
PART 3 ・ 特殊比較句型

越來越、越…越…

情境

「你練習得越多，就會越有自信。」這個句型在中譯英出現的機會非常高。

The more you practice, the more confident you will become.

觀察

兩半都是「the + 比較級」放在句首，後面再接主詞和動詞。最常見的錯誤是把形容詞留在原位：the more you will become confident。

規律 → 特殊句型
意思句型例句
越…就越…The + 比較級 + S + V, the + 比較級 + S + VThe earlier you start, the better your results will be.
越來越…比較級 + and + 比較級The weather is getting hotter and hotter.
Online shopping is becoming more and more popular.
比其他任何…都比較級 + than any other + 單數名詞Mt. Jade is higher than any other mountain in Taiwan.
沒有比…更No other + 單數名詞 + is + 比較級 + thanNo other mountain in Taiwan is higher than Mt. Jade.
寧願 A 也不 Bprefer A to B / would rather A than BI prefer tea to coffee. / I'd rather walk than take a taxi.
PART 4 ・ 連接詞與平行結構

連起來的東西要長得一樣

情境

I like swimming, reading, and to play the guitar. 三件事用 and 連起來，形式卻一個是 V-ing、一個是 to V。

觀察

用連接詞連接的部分，詞性和形式要一致，這叫「平行結構」：I like swimming, reading, and playing the guitar. 這是作文「文法句構」看得出程度的地方。

規律 → 相關連接詞與動詞一致
連接詞動詞跟誰一致例句
both A and B複數Both my sister and I are students.
either A or B / neither A nor B跟 B（靠近動詞的那個）Neither the students nor the teacher was late.
not only A but (also) B跟 BNot only the kids but also their mom loves the show.
A as well as B跟 AThe teacher, as well as the students, was excited.
A 和 B 也要平行。She is not only smart but also hardworking.（形容詞對形容詞）；You can either call me or send me a message.（動詞對動詞）。
練習

動手試試看

Q1中譯英：天氣越來越冷了。
看解答
The weather is getting colder and colder.（或 It is getting colder and colder.）
Q2改正這句：

The weather in Tainan is warmer than Taipei.

看解答
The weather in Tainan is warmer than that in Taipei. 比較的是天氣和天氣。
Q3中譯英：你越常閱讀，你的英文就會越好。
看解答
The more often you read, the better your English will become.
Q4選出正確的動詞：

Neither my parents nor my brother ______ going to the concert.

  1. is
  2. are
  3. were
  4. have
看解答
A。neither A nor B 的動詞跟 B（my brother）一致，用單數。
Q5改成平行結構：

To stay healthy, you should eat well, sleeping enough, and exercise.

看解答
To stay healthy, you should eat well, sleep enough, and exercise. should 後面三個都用原形動詞。
本節重點
  • 倍數：three times as … as ＝ three times …er than；兩倍用 twice。
  • 比較對象要一致，用 that / those 代替；修飾比較級用 much、far、even。
  • 越…越…：The 比較級 S V, the 比較級 S V；越來越：比較級 and 比較級。
  • either/neither/not only 的動詞跟 B；as well as 跟 A。
  • 連接詞連接的部分要平行。

例句皆為自編教學用。
