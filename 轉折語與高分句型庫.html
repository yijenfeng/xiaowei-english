第參部分・英文作文・2-4

轉折語與高分句型庫

這一頁是作文的工具箱，不用一次背完。依段落的功能分類，每一類挑兩三個自己最順手的句型，練到寫得出來、不會拼錯就好。考前一週，這一頁和錯題本是最值得反覆看的兩樣東西。

這一頁回答同一個問題的五個面向：「寫到這裡卡住了，下一句可以怎麼開頭？」

  • 1開頭與描述
  • 2原因、結果、舉例
  • 3對比與讓步
  • 4經驗、感想、結論
  • 5句子升級
  • ✎改寫練習
PART 1 ・ 開頭與描述

第一句就讓讀者知道主題

用途句型例
描述現象These days, more and more people …These days, more and more people keep pets in small apartments.
描述現象It is common to see … / It is not unusual for … to …It is common to see students studying in cafés.
描述圖片As shown in the picture, … / The picture shows …As shown in the picture, a crowd has gathered outside the store.
說故事One + 時間 + , … / It was … when …It was almost midnight when I heard a knock at the door.
表明立場As far as I am concerned, … / In my opinion, …As far as I am concerned, homework should be limited.
PART 2 ・ 原因、結果、舉例

讓內容有深度的三種句子

用途句型例
原因One reason is that … / This is mainly because …This is mainly because rent in big cities is high.
原因… is due to / is a result of …The delay was due to heavy rain.
結果As a result, … / Consequently, …As a result, many shops closed early.
結果… lead to / result in …Too much screen time can lead to poor sleep.
因果強調so + adj + that … / such + a + adj + N + that …The test was so long that I barely finished it.
舉例For example, … / Take … as an example.Take my brother as an example.
舉例…, such as A, B, and Coutdoor activities, such as hiking and cycling
PART 3 ・ 對比與讓步

看見另一面，論述更成熟

用途句型例
對比However, … / On the other hand, …On the other hand, pets require time and money.
對比A …, while B …Some people enjoy the day off, while others have to work harder.
讓步Although / Even though S V, S VAlthough the rule seems strict, it protects students.
讓步Despite / In spite of + N / V-ingDespite the cost, many families choose to travel.
承認＋反駁It is true that …; however, …It is true that phones are useful; however, they are also distracting.
PART 4 ・ 經驗、感想、結論

第二段和最後一句的好幫手

用途句型例
帶出經驗I still remember the time when …I still remember the time when I got lost in Taipei Main Station.
回顧反省Looking back, I realize that … / I should have …Looking back, I realize that asking for help is not a weakness.
學到什麼The experience taught me that … / I learned that …The experience taught me that kindness can come from strangers.
假設If I were …, I would … / Had I known …, I would have …If I were the principal, I would build more bike racks.
結論All in all, … / In conclusion, … / For these reasons, …All in all, a small change in habit can make a big difference.
建議It would be better if … / We should … instead of …We should talk to each other instead of staring at screens.
PART 5 ・ 句子升級

同一個意思，寫得更有層次

觀察

「文法句構」看的不只是有沒有錯，還有句型變化。把兩三個簡單句合成一個複句，是最直接的升級方式。下面每一組都用到前面文法教材的句型：

原句升級後用到
I was tired. I went to bed early.Feeling tired, I went to bed early.分詞構句（文法 1-2）
I have a friend. Her father is a pilot.I have a friend whose father is a pilot.關係子句（文法 2-1）
I didn't know the truth. I trusted him.Had I known the truth, I would not have trusted him.假設倒裝（文法 1-3）
I have never seen such a sunset.Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.否定倒裝（文法 2-3）
You practice more. You become more confident.The more you practice, the more confident you become.越…越（文法 2-2）
不要每句都升級。一篇作文有三到四個漂亮的複句就很夠了。簡單句寫對，加上幾個有把握的高分句型，比整篇硬塞難句安全。
改寫練習

把兩句合成一句

作答後點選答案或按「對答案」，作答紀錄會保留在這台裝置。
1The movie was very boring. Half of the audience fell asleep.（用 so … that）
看標準答案與解析
The movie was so boring that half of the audience fell asleep.
2It rained heavily. We still finished the race.（用 Despite）
看標準答案與解析
Despite the heavy rain, we still finished the race.
3I helped the old lady. I felt happy.（用 Looking back 或分詞構句）
看標準答案與解析
Having helped the old lady, I felt happy for the rest of the day. 或 Looking back, helping that old lady made me truly happy.
本節重點
  • 依功能分類：開頭、原因結果、舉例、對比讓步、經驗、結論。
  • 每類挑兩三個自己會拼、會用的句型就好。
  • 一篇作文三到四個漂亮複句，其餘寫對的簡單句。

例句皆為自編教學用。
